One Level Deeper Understanding the layers below a problem to make the right engineering trade-offs.

I'm a software and systems engineer with over a decade of experience building across Linux and Windows environments. Throughout my career, I've delivered systems for the defense, government, healthcare, financial, and marketing automation sectors. I believe in understanding at least one layer beneath any problem I'm solving, working across programming languages, cloud infrastructure, networking, and operating system internals to make sound architectural trade-offs.

Alongside my civilian career, I served for 12 years in the U.S. military. I spent my first six years in the U.S. Army focusing on munitions logistics, with additional assignments in theater-level intelligence and IT support. I then transitioned to the U.S. Air Force, where I specialized in radio frequency (RF) systems, leading spectrum management, solutions engineering, asset readiness, and regulatory compliance across federal, military, and international standards.

Off the clock, I gravitate toward unfamiliar technical territory and low-level systems. I spend much of my free time exploring operating system internals, compilers and language runtimes, graphics programming, game development, and offensive security.
